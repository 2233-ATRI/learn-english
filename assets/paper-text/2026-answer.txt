environments serve as vital, unstructured classrooms where
children learn through discovery and sensory engagement.
Meanwhile,  the  recognition  of  parent-child  interaction
highlights an understanding of the relational and emotional
benefits derived from shared outdoor experiences. In an age
dominated by  digital screens, these findings underscore a
collective yearning for reconnection with the natural world as
a foundation for holistic growth.
